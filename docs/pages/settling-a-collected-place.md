title:: Settling a Place we have already collected on
type:: policy

- Collecting on a proposed [[place]] is allowed, so the question of what becomes of that collection when the Place is later settled has to be answered.
- A [[post]]'s where is not stored on the Post; it is read from the Post's current [[placement]]. So settling a Place never rewrites a judgment: the Post keeps the placement it was given, and what that placement now *means* is resolved when we read it.
- Whichever way the Place is settled, no Post is ever left reading as a Place that is no longer part of the [[event]]:
  - **Promoted** — the Place was real. Its placements stand as they are, and nothing moves.
  - **Deprecated** — the Place turned out to be another Place under a different name. A placement at it reads as its **replacement**, followed onwards if that Place was itself deprecated.
  - **Discarded** — the Place was not real, and there is no replacement to follow. A placement at it reads as **Unplaced**: we know we collected the Post and no longer know where it is about. See [[unplaced-place]]. An [[actor]] may make a fresh placement to relocate the Post to a Place that is real.
- This is what [[open-placement]] was waiting on; once [[placement]] is built, this policy is settled.
