title:: Person
type:: concept

- A **person** is a human the [[event]]'s story is about — whom a [[claim]] is *about*, never the [[actor]] who *made* it. Broadly: victims (trapped, rescued, missing, dead) and families, rescuers, officials and politicians, reporters and public figures. (Non-human notables — the rescue dogs — and organizations carry no privacy and are simply named in a [[claim]]; if they too should carry arcs, this widens to a **Figure**. The name is provisional.) One person belongs to one [[event]].
- **Private or public sets everything else.** A **public** person acting publicly — official, politician, reporter, public figure — is named as the posts name them. A **private** person — a victim, an ordinary rescuer, a bystander — is protected, never named raw. Which one is a human's call.
- **A private person is named only by a handle.** Their full name is kept behind a **human gate** — the key that recognizes them across [[post]]s and [[claim]]s — and is never written into a [[claim]] or a [[beat]]. What shows is a **display handle**: first name + last-name initial ("Aaron C.") beside the role, down a ladder to the first name, then to **role alone** when the name cannot be resolved with confidence (there, plus a flag for a human — the system never guesses a name; [[principle-a-wrong-answer-is-worse-than-none]]). A **minor** is always role-only: no child's name is shown or stored.
- **A private person-status account is gated.** Trapped, rescued, missing, dead is the highest-stakes thing this system says, so no such [[claim]] is public until a human **approves** the person; **withhold** is the operator's to set.
- **A person links their [[claim]]s into an arc** — a disappearance then a rescue or a death; a rescuer across those they reached — one story across time. This is not [[claim]] **merge**, which folds claims about one *happening* into one: a person **links** claims about one *individual* across happenings.
