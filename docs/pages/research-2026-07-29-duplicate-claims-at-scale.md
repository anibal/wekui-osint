title:: Research — 3,213 duplicate proposals, and the four instruments that lied
type:: research

- The duplicate finder had 2,596 current [[claim]]s to compare and offered **3,213 pairs**. That is not a review, it is a second job, and the operator's standing rule forbids handing him one. This measures what the pairs were actually made of, and it found four defects — three of them deterministic, none of them needing a model to fix.
- **First: 3,213 pairs were 261 groups.** The finder is pairwise because the rule in [[claim]] is pairwise; a corpus is not. Twelve groups held **77% of every pair**, and one clique of 61 support claims held **1,516 pairs by itself — 47% of the whole queue** restating one fact. 160 groups were a single genuine pair. `Duplicates.cluster/1` joins the pairs back into the groups they describe, and the group is what a person is now given. A group is a **candidate, not an assertion**: `equipo USAR El Salvador` and `equipo USAR de Perú` are two teams and both join a generic `equipo extranjero`, so one group holds all three.
- **Second: two absent subjects scored a PERFECT match.** `String.jaro_distance("", "")` is 1.0, and 1,223 of 2,596 claims name no subject — a building collapse's subject is the building, which is a [[place]]. So **2,467 pairs, 77% of the findings, rested on nothing and sorted above every pair resting on something**. The number was never wrong; it was answering a question nobody asked. A finding now says what it rests on — `:mark`, `:generic`, `:absent` — and that orders the queue ahead of the score.
- **Third: the record already knew WHO, and nobody asked it.** Two women of 60 went missing in one building. Both claims read *"una mujer de 60 años"*, scored 1.0, and **two independent adversarial readings agreed they were one happening**. The [[person]] rows behind them said `gladismaria pineda ramirez` and `mirta guedez` the whole time. Comparing the persons a claim names refuses **122 of 3,213 pairs**, and five of those the model had upheld: five people who would have been merged away.
  - The test had to be **recall over the shorter name's own words**, not whole-string distance — the [[theme]] resolver's lesson, and the corpus needs it. One woman held **four Person rows**: `belkys josefina barreto garcia`, `belkis josefina barreto garcia`, `belkys barreto`, `belkis barreto`. Whole-string they read 0.70 apart; by words, 0.944. A dropped middle name is a matter of how much someone wrote down, not of who they meant.
  - It fires only when **both** claims name someone. An unlinked claim asserts nothing about identity, and absence is never disagreement — the same line already drawn for a blank subject and for an unaudited residue entry.
- **Fourth: the place that held everything told nothing.** 380 of 2,596 claims sit at «Caraballeda» the parroquia and 62 at «La Guaira» the estado, because that is where the whole corpus is. Counting that as agreement built the 61-claim support clique and a 35-claim collapse clique — between them **1,740 pairs saying only that everything happened in Caraballeda**. Requiring a shared place finer than a parroquia cut 3,120 pairs to **1,099** and the largest group from 61 accounts to 19. `barrio` and `sector` are deliberately left in: cutting them too gave 989 pairs, and the finder's job is recall while the gate is downstream.
- **The whole queue, end to end: 3,213 pairs in 261 groups became 1,033 pairs in 129 groups**, and the groups at the top are now real — 19 accounts of one boy's rescue, 16 accounts of one hotel's collapse. Each is one decision, not 171.
- **What the adversarial judge was worth, and where it is invalid.** Two independent readings of the 160 standing-alone pairs, the second with A and B swapped to defeat positional bias: **94 withdrawn (59%), 42 upheld (26%), 24 split (15%)**. Only a unanimous SAME upholds; a split stays a split, because rolling a third reading until two agree is picking the run that reads cleanest. But **only 4 of the 42 upheld pairs rested on a mark** — the rest rested on a generic or absent subject, which is where it made its five person-merging errors. And inside a dense group pairwise judging cannot mean anything: refute one edge and the group survives through any other path. Twelve groups held 77% of the pairs, so judging every pair would have spent nearly half the money on the one shape where the answer is void.
- **A receipt outlives the finder it ran against, and must not take credit for rules that landed after it.** The judge ran before the person gate and the scope rule existed. Of its 42 upheld pairs **6 are refused today by rule** — five by the persons they name — and of its 94 withdrawals **28** would never have been offered. The stale entries are inert, because the report subtracts the receipt from what the finder offers *now*; what needed fixing was the sentence, which credited the model with 94 removals on a queue where it accounts for 66. **A number measured against one instrument is not a fact about a later one.**
- **The pattern, and it is the same one as last time.** Every defect here was an instrument reporting a number instead of reporting what it was doing: a similarity score for subjects that did not exist, a place match for a place that contains everything, a pair count where a group count was the fact. [[research-2026-07-29-sweeping-the-corpus]] found seven losses the same way, five of them inside instruments built to find losses. **Three of the four fixes were rules the system had already written down somewhere else** — the pair-judge prompt's own *"a parish named in both is nearly worthless"*, the vocabulary's *"a woman missing in one building and a woman missing in another are two claims"*, the theme resolver's word-recall — sitting at rung five when they belonged at rung one.
