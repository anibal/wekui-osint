title:: Decision — a person's act is a record, not a stamp
type:: decision

- A person's deliberate act on the record is written as its **own append-only row** — a [[curation]] act carrying the [[actor]] who decided, when, what changed, and why — and **not** as `decided_by` / `decided_at` fields stamped onto the thing that was curated.
- **What decided it:** a stamp answers *who last touched this*, and only that. The second correction overwrites the first, so *"what did he change on the 27th"* has no answer — and that is [[principle-never-rewrite-the-record]] failing in the one place the project can least afford it, given that auditability is the whole thesis. The record's own symmetry made the call: the system's acts had just earned a receipt in [[run]], and a human's acts deserve no less.
- **What was set aside, and why it is worth naming:** reusing the [[judgment]] spine. It has exactly the right *shape* — an Actor's answer, append-only, superseding, provenance-carrying — but it is Post-and-Author-scoped, and stretching it over Places, Persons and Claims would have cost more than it saved. The resemblance is real and was weighed, not missed.
- **How a person is named:** an [[actor]] of kind person carries a `name`, upserted on (event, name), so the same name is the same curator. That is the whole of a person's details — the question [[open-actor]] left open, answered by the smallest thing that works. An Actor's name is never a [[person]]'s name and the private-name gate does not reach it: a Person is whom a [[claim]] is *about*, an Actor is who *acted*.
- **An act and its change are one transaction.** Neither lands without the other, because a change with no act is precisely the unattributed act this decision abolishes.
- **A claim–place link may now be dropped**, which nothing else in the model permits. It is allowed because the link is the one deliberately mutable thing here — a current best reading, not a historical fact — and because the act that drops it preserves what it read, with who dropped it and why. The information moves into the audit trail rather than out of the record.
- **The debt this decision does not pay.** Three curation acts were applied on 2026-07-27 *before* any of this existed: two [[place]]s reparented, one [[claim]] linked by hand, five [[person]]s approved. **None carries a who**, and none ever will. Retro-attributing them — minting an Actor row and back-dating it — would be exactly the rewriting this decision is built to prevent. Their record is the committed script that applied them, `priv/scripts/curate_2026_07_27.exs`, and this bullet.
- Evidence: `docs/curation-scenarios.md` (the shapes weighed, and the tree query that answered the two-Caraballedas question), and the acts themselves in `curation_acts`.
