- Measurements and evidence: what the [[decision-log]] cites. Research runs are born at a moment and dated in their names; long-form prose write-ups live at `docs/` root, and the dated page here is their outline summary.
- [[research-2026-07-23-old-app-corpus]] — the old app's real database; the evidence base behind the decision log.
- [[research-2026-07-23-clustering-spike]] — telling Posts apart, and the missing-person trap; informs the open dedup question.
- [[research-2026-07-27-extraction-on-real-posts]] — the extractor read real posts for the first time and asserted things they do not say in 12 of 26 claims; two prompt rules cut that to 3 of 20.
- [[research-2026-07-29-sweeping-the-corpus]] — 2,800 posts read in batches; zero dead vocabulary, residue down to ~1 per 100 after auditing, and seven losses found only by making the instruments say what they were doing.
- [[research-2026-07-27-three-readers-one-taxonomy]] — three independent readers of the same 302 posts converged 58–67% above chance and reached ten of the same [[theme]]s; the 2% they could not name turned out to be a modality gap, not a vocabulary one.
- [[research-2026-07-29-telling-people-apart]] — three ways to tell one person from two; the first was falsified because context has a SIGN, and in a disaster "same building, same day" means family. The winner scored 20/22 twice and was the only one that repeated itself.
- [[research-2026-07-29-duplicate-claims-at-scale]] — 3,213 duplicate proposals were 261 groups, 77% rested on subjects that did not exist, and two adversarial readings merged five people the Person rows could already tell apart; four instruments lied and three of the fixes were rules the system had already written down elsewhere.
