title:: Decision — the corpus crosses whole, and the rehearsal nine stand
type:: decision

- Porting the old app's [[post]]s into this record carries the **payload X sent, unaltered**, and reads `text` out of it. The old app's `normalized_text` — lowercased and accent-folded for matching — is never the text of a Post, and its `place_judgments` are never carried at all: they choose *which* posts to bring, and nothing more. Placement here is a [[claim]]'s property, resolved fresh from what the posts say.
- **What the port found, measured on all nine.** The posts this record has stood on since 2026-07-25 are not collected posts. Their `x_id` is the old app's **row id** (`"129"`), not the X post id (`"2070003831404908894"`), and their text is **editorially rewritten** — not merely cleaned. Every one of the nine is shorter than the post it stands for; together they are **1,732 characters against 3,788**, 46% of what was actually written.
  - What the rewriting removes is exactly what the extractor must survive: the leading `@mention` and the `🚨` and the `|` separator, the trailing `t.co` URL, the paragraph breaks, the quoted speech, the hashtags, the donation solicitation with its crypto addresses.
  - It also removes **facts**. The rehearsal text of post 3920 drops the sentence that a body lies between the rescuers and Aaron; the rehearsal text of 4746 (123 characters against 871) keeps the official toll and drops that volunteers were still clearing rubble by hand in Caraballeda. Two happenings the real posts evidence cannot be extracted from the rehearsal at all.
- **So every measurement of extraction so far was taken on prose that had already been tidied of the noise it has to read.** The 8 claims, the v5 prompt's eight-of-nine citation rate, the beat — all of it stands on a rehearsal. That is not a reason to distrust the claims; it is the reason the corpus had to arrive.
- **The nine stay as they are.** A Post is never edited and never deleted, and 8 [[claim]]s and 19 [[curation]] acts rest on them. The port SKIPS a post the record already holds under its row id, so the nine are not doubled, and the real corpus arrives beside them under true X ids.
  - The wart is named rather than buried: a live acquisition would collect those same nine messages again under their true ids, as new Posts, and the record would then hold each happening twice. `INCLUDE_HELD=1` brings them across deliberately instead — an open choice, not a default.
- **The port widens what the record holds, not what a reader reaches.** It brought 79 real [[author]] accounts and their full X payloads across — the first time this record holds either at scale, since the rehearsal payloads were synthetic `{"text", "id"}` stubs. An Author is a Capture row: nothing in the beat, the report or any reader-facing surface renders one. The private-name gate lives on [[person]] — whom a [[claim]] is *about* — and it is exactly where it was. The obligation this creates is forward: whatever review console is built must not put an Author's handle beside a claim without deciding to.
- **What this makes measurable — and does not yet answer.** Whether v5 survives the real thing is now an experiment the record can run. It has not been run: no ruling has reached a prompt yet.
- Evidence: `priv/scripts/port_corpus.exs`; the nine rehearsal texts against their payloads, character for character; the 98 posts it brought into the 2026-06-25 04:00–05:00 hour beside the 2 rehearsal posts it skipped.
