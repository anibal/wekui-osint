title:: A merge is a deprecation, not a judgment of its own
type:: decision

- **Decision** — there is no separate merge concept. When two [[place]]s (or [[theme]]s) turn out to be the same, one is **deprecated** onto the other, and every [[post]] placed at it reads through the replacement — see [[settling-a-collected-place]] and [[placement]]. Nothing writes a "merge".
- The old design had a third provenance mode, `merge`, that rewrote judgments when two nodes were combined.
- It was never used: zero merge rows across every judgment table in the corpus. The lifecycle already says "this turned out to be that" — a deprecation with a replacement — and the where-read follows the replacement, so a merge writes nothing new.
- Telling apart two Posts that report the same happening is a different question, deferred on purpose — see [[open-when-two-posts-say-the-same-thing]].
