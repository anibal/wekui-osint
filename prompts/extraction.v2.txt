## A claim = one happening

A collapse, a rescue, a disappearance, an official toll, aid arriving, infrastructure lost/restored. ONE claim per happening — 50 posts about one rescue is ONE claim citing many. VOLUME IS NOT NOVELTY.

A post yields 0, 1, or SEVERAL claims. A happening can hide inside another's post — a missing-person post that also reveals their building collapsed is TWO claims, one per happening.

DISTINCT people are DISTINCT claims even under identical wording — tell them apart by attribute (age, floor, occupation, relation), never merge on a shared template. People reported together as a unit ("a mother and her child") may be one claim.

## Fields

- "kind": rescate | desaparicion | colapso | cifra_oficial | ayuda | reporte_vecinal | otro.

- "subject_role": who the claim is about, by ROLE + ATTRIBUTE — sex, age, occupation, relationship, floor, building — NEVER by name: "un hombre de 21 años", "una maestra", "un niño". null when no person (a collapse, a toll). A PRIVATE individual is a role, always. A PUBLIC figure acting publicly (an official, a politician, a public personality) may be named here — UNLESS naming them would identify a private relative (a public figure's parent/child/sibling): then drop the public figure entirely and give the relative's role only, with no link back to the public figure anywhere in the claim.

- "person_name": private individuals' full name(s), exactly as a post gives them, as an ARRAY — always an array, even for one person: ["Julio Josué Freitas Rodríguez"], or ["Yaneth Tejera", "Shaznay Mirabal"] for a reported pair. This feeds the PROTECTED identity: stored behind a human gate, NEVER shown to a reader. The only empty value is [] — never null. Use [] when: no name is given; the subject is a public figure acting publicly (they're a source, not a protected subject); or the individual is a MINOR — a minor's name is NEVER recorded here, even when the post names them: "Lucas, el chico de 9 años" → subject_role "un niño de 9 años", person_name [].

- "place_mention": the most specific place THE POST ITSELF names for this happening — "edificio OPP 25", "Residencias Caribe, piso 9" — even if broader or narrower than {{place_scope}}. Never substitute the batch's scope for what the text says. null if none. (Gazetteer-matched afterward, not by you.)

- "magnitude": the numbers the evidence carries, as a small object — {"fallecidos": 1943, "heridos": 10571}, {"piso": 9}. Elapsed time since t0 is derivable and belongs here — a post timestamped ~106h after t0 whose subject "permanece atrapado" gets {"horas": 106}, rounded to the hour, never to the minute. Age belongs in subject_role's phrase, not here. Invent no other number the material does not contain.

- "status": rescatado | desaparecido | fallecido | atrapado | en_curso | null — the CURRENTLY EVIDENCED standing, independent of "kind". An ongoing search is kind=rescate but status=atrapado or en_curso until an outcome is actually reported: "labores de búsqueda y rescate... permanece atrapado" is status atrapado, NOT rescatado — the word "rescate" in the text names the operation, not the outcome. Set rescatado/fallecido only when the text states that outcome.

- "citations": post ids evidencing this claim, STRONGEST FIRST. Strongest = most specific and most directly evidencing (a firsthand account or official statement over a repost); ties break to earliest effective time. The first citation's effective time stamps the claim's moment.

- "confidence": how faithfully the citations support this exact claim, on this ladder —
  - 0.4 — a single, vague, secondhand plea ("aún tenemos esperanzas…"); phrase subject_role as a report ("vecinos reportan…"), not as fact.
  - 0.6 — a single post, thin on detail or informal in tone.
  - 0.75 — a single post that is direct, specific, and formal (a firsthand account, a named institution, a formal missing-person notice).
  - 0.9 — an official bulletin, OR the same happening independently corroborated by two or more authors.
  Copy-paste or reposts of one text count as ONE source, not many.

## Discipline

- EVIDENCE ONLY, at the strength shown: state as fact only what the citations show. No outside knowledge, no interpolation between posts, no assumed outcomes.
- Empty list is a correct answer when the material is pure repetition, off-scope, or noise (real-estate chatter, general commentary) — do not force a claim.

## Output

Output exactly one JSON object, nothing else — no prose before or after, no markdown fence. Emit "kind" and "status" values verbatim (Spanish) — never translate them.
