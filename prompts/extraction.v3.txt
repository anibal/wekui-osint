## Three rules that are never broken

1. NO NAME EVER in "subject_role" — not the person's, not a relative's, not a public figure's. In "subject_role" a person is only a ROLE and its attributes: "un hombre de 21 años", "una maestra", "un niño". A relationship to UNNAMED people is fine ("una mujer y sus dos hijos"); a relationship to a NAMED person is NOT — "el padre de la pianista Gabriela Montero" is forbidden, write "un hombre". Names go only in the "names" field.

2. A MINOR is never named anywhere — role in "subject_role", empty "names", even when the post names them: "Lucas, el chico de 9 años" → subject_role "un niño de 9 años", names [].

3. ONE claim per HAPPENING, but hold every happening. 50 posts about one rescue is ONE claim citing many — volume is not novelty. Yet DISTINCT people are DISTINCT claims even in identical wording (tell them apart by attribute, never merge on a shared template); a post may hold SEVERAL happenings (a missing-person post that also says their building collapsed is TWO claims); and every person reported missing, trapped, rescued, or dead is a claim — even one named only in passing ("Aún no hay noticias de Lucas" is its own claim).

## The fields of a claim

- "kind": rescate | desaparicion | colapso | cifra_oficial | ayuda | reporte_vecinal | otro.
- "subject_role": the role + attributes of who the claim is about (rule 1) — sex, age, occupation, floor, building. null when the claim is about no person (a collapse, a toll).
- "names": the full name(s) the posts give for the person(s) this claim is ABOUT — not the sources who reported it — as an array: ["Julio Josué Freitas Rodríguez"], or two names for a reported pair. [] when no name is given, when the subject is a group without names, or for a MINOR (rule 2). Used only to recognize the person behind a gate; never shown to a reader.
- "place_mention": the most specific place the POST itself names for this happening — "edificio OPP 25", "Residencias Caribe, piso 9" — even if broader or narrower than {{place_scope}}. null if none.
- "magnitude": the numbers the evidence carries — {"fallecidos": 1943, "heridos": 10571}, {"piso": 9}. Hours-since-t0 belong here for someone still trapped: a post ~106h after t0 whose subject "permanece atrapado" → {"horas": 106} (whole hours). Age goes in subject_role, not here. Invent no other number.
- "status": rescatado | desaparecido | fallecido | atrapado | en_curso | null — the standing the text actually states, INDEPENDENT of kind. "labores de rescate… permanece atrapado" is atrapado, not rescatado — "rescate" names the operation, not the outcome. Use rescatado or fallecido only when the text states that outcome.
- "citations": the ids that evidence this claim, strongest first (most specific and direct; ties break to earliest time). The first citation's effective time stamps the claim's moment.
- "confidence": 0.9 an official bulletin OR two-plus independent authors · 0.75 one direct, specific, formal source · 0.6 one thin or informal source · 0.4 a single vague plea (and phrase subject_role as a report — "vecinos reportan…"). Reposts of one text count as one source.

Evidence only, at the strength shown — no outside knowledge, no interpolation, no assumed outcomes. Pure repetition, off-scope, or noise (real-estate chatter) → return an empty list.

Output exactly one JSON object, no prose and no markdown fence. Emit "kind" and "status" verbatim in Spanish.
