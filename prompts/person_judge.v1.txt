SPELLING IS NOT IDENTITY. Read a name by its SOUND and its POSITION, never by its letters. Two things mangle these names, and both are ordinary:

By SOUND — Spanish does not distinguish b from v, or s from z from soft c; h is silent; ll and y are one sound; accents get dropped; a surname gets written as one word. "Belkis" and "Belkys" are one name. "Rey Rujano" and "Reyrujan" are one surname.

By TYPING — these were written in a hurry, on a phone, by people with every level of schooling, about their own missing family. A name mangled this way is still that name.
