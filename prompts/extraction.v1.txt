## What a claim is

ONE claim per HAPPENING — a distinct state change: a collapse, a rescue, a disappearance, an official toll, aid arriving, infrastructure lost or restored. VOLUME IS NOT NOVELTY: fifty posts about one rescue are ONE claim that cites many. A post may yield ZERO, one, or SEVERAL claims.

DISTINCT people are distinct claims, even when the wording is identical. Two people reported missing in the same building are two claims — tell them apart by their attributes, and never merge them on a shared template. (One report of two people together — "a mother and her child" — may be one claim.)

## The fields of each claim

- "kind": one of  rescate | desaparicion | colapso | cifra_oficial | ayuda | reporte_vecinal | otro.

- "subject_role": the person or people the claim is about, told by ROLE AND ATTRIBUTE — sex, age, occupation, relationship, floor, building — and NEVER by name: "un hombre de 21 años", "una maestra", "un niño", "una mujer y sus dos hijos". Use null when the claim is about no person (a collapse, a toll). A PRIVATE individual is only ever a role here — never a name. The one exception: a PUBLIC figure acting publicly (an official, a politician, a public personality) may be named here as the posts name them.

- "person_name": ONLY for a private individual the claim is about — their full name exactly as a post gives it. This feeds the record's PROTECTED identity: it is stored behind a human gate and is NEVER shown to a reader. Use null when no name is given, when the subject is a group, or when the individual is a MINOR (never record a child's name). This is the ONLY field in which a private name may appear. Public officials and figures acting publicly are NOT recorded here — they are named in a post as sources, not as the claim's subject.

- "place_mention": the most specific place the claim is about, as the posts name it — "edificio OPP 25, Tanaguarena", "Residencias Caribe". null if none. (It is matched to the gazetteer afterward, not by you.)

- "magnitude": the numbers the evidence carries, as a small object — {"horas": 106}, {"fallecidos": 1943, "heridos": 10571}, {"piso": 9}. null if none. Invent no number the material does not contain.

- "status": the standing the evidence shows — rescatado | desaparecido | fallecido | atrapado | en_curso — or null.

- "citations": the ids of the posts that genuinely evidence this claim, STRONGEST FIRST. The first citation's effective time stamps the claim's moment. Cite what you used, and nothing you did not.

- "confidence": how sure you are that the claim faithfully represents what the cited posts show, 0 to 1 — NOT how dramatic it is. A single-source claim is lower, and its subject_role is phrased as a report ("vecinos reportan…"); independent authors raise it. Copy-paste of one text is one source, not many.

## Discipline

- EVIDENCE ONLY, at the strength shown. Attribute an uncorroborated claim to its source ("según un usuario…", "vecinos reportan…"); state as fact only what independent posts corroborate. No outside knowledge, no interpolation between posts, no assumed outcomes, no invented precision.

- A public figure may be named in a post, but never in a way that identifies a private person: if naming a public figure would reveal who a private individual is (a public figure's relative), leave the public figure out entirely and give only the role.

- If the material holds NO new state change — pure repetition of the known story, off-scope chatter, noise — return an empty list. That is the correct answer, not a failure.
